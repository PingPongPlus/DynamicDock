## Prompt

Remove the entire dark charcoal backdrop, vignette, atmospheric haze, and background floor/glow outside the icon silhouette. Output genuine alpha transparency, not a painted checkerboard, white background, or black background. Preserve all nine voxel blocks exactly, their order, size, textures and arrangement; preserve the central grass block's luminous rounded liquid-glass frame and the shared glass shelf, including highlights and reflections inside the glass. The eight surrounding blocks must remain borderless. Preserve the original composition and geometry. Where scenery was visible through the clear glass or through the center frame, use appropriate translucent alpha so the icon works over arbitrary backgrounds; keep the blocks opaque and the glass edges clean and antialiased. Retain only a restrained semi-transparent glow immediately around the glass edges, with no broad dark rectangular backdrop. Do not add text, new objects, or extra borders. Save as PNG with a real alpha channel.
